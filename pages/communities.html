MindBridge Community Guidelines

Our shared agreement for maintaining an empathetic space.

1

Respect & Emotional Safety: We do not tolerate invalidation, harassment, bullying, discrimination, or aggressive debate.

2

Confidentiality: What is shared in discussions stays here. Never screenshot, repost, or disclose another student's vulnerable disclosures.

3

No Clinical Diagnosis: Offer support, validation, and peer perspective—not unsolicited psychiatric diagnoses or prescription advice.

4

Crisis Protocol: If a post suggests immediate physical danger or suicidal intent, report it immediately so campus crisis liaisons can safely intervene.
