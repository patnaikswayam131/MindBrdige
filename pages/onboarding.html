Privacy-First Mental Wellness

Welcome to MindBridge.

MindBridge is built exclusively to support student mental health and emotional well-being. Before we begin, we want to be completely transparent about how your information is handled.

Private & Isolated

Your private journal entries and mood check-ins are strictly isolated to your account. We never sell your personal data to advertisers.

Full Control in Settings

You can review, modify, export (as JSON archive), or permanently wipe your local records at any time directly through Settings.

Important Clinical Notice: MindBridge provides peer support, guided reflections, and self-management resources. It is not an emergency psychiatric facility or a substitute for licensed medical care. If you are experiencing a life-threatening crisis, please dial 988 or 1800-599-0019.
