About MindBridge

MindBridge is a student-focused digital mental-health support platform designed to make mental-health resources and professional support easier to access.

The platform brings together AI assistance, mental-health check-ins, professional counseling, appointments, secure communication, and wellbeing resources into one unified experience tailored for college life.

  • Dedicated strictly to university students and young adults
  • Designed around privacy and secure communication
  • Calm, minimal, and pressure-free environment
